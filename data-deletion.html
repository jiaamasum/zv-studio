User Data Deletion

Last updated: November 19, 2026

The ZV Studio publishing app is used only by ZV Studio for its own YouTube channels and Facebook Page. It does not collect personal data from Facebook users. The only Facebook data it keeps is the Page access token of ZV Studio's own Page and the view/like counts of Reels it published.

How to delete your data

  1. Go to Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  2. Find the app and click Remove. Facebook revokes the app's access immediately.
  3. Once access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from ZV Studio's computer, together with any data linked to it, within 30 days.

To ask for deletion of anything else, message us through the About page of our YouTube channels or our Facebook Page.
